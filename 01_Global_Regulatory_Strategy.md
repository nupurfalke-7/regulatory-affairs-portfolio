# Global Regulatory Strategy

## Product profile
- Product: NUP-MET 500 mg
- API: Metformin Hydrochloride
- Dosage form: Immediate-release film-coated tablet
- Route: Oral
- Therapeutic area: Type 2 diabetes
- Regulatory category: Simulated generic pharmaceutical product

## Strategy
The simulated strategy compares the regulatory documentation and submission considerations for India, the US, EU and UK. The project emphasizes CTD/eCTD structure, regional Module 1 requirements, quality documentation, labeling and lifecycle management.

## Risk themes
1. Regional administrative requirements may differ.
2. Quality/CMC information must be internally consistent across dossier sections.
3. Labeling must match the applicable regional requirements.
4. Submission documents require version control and traceability.
5. Regulatory intelligence should be monitored for guideline changes.
