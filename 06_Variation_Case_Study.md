# Mock Variation / Change-Control Case Study

## Scenario
A fictional manufacturer proposes a change to a secondary packaging component for NUP-MET 500 mg.

## Regulatory assessment
1. Identify the affected dossier sections.
2. Assess whether the change can affect product quality or stability.
3. Determine the applicable regional reporting category using current official guidance.
4. Update affected documents and maintain version traceability.
5. Record the regulatory intelligence/review decision.

## RA deliverables
- Change-control assessment
- Affected CTD sections list
- Supporting-document checklist
- Submission/reporting decision
- Lifecycle tracking entry
